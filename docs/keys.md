# Keys

Every key the app takes, by where you are. The bottom strip of the window
shows the ones that work where you are now.

```
⌘K               palette: open anything by name, or run a command
click            open a folder or document in the active tab
⌘-click          open in a new tab      ⌘⇧-click   open in the next pane
F2 / dbl-click   rename                 right-click  new, import, rename, move to, delete,
                                                     the same in the sidebar and the folder lens;
                                                     anywhere else there, the folder's own
+                beside the vault's name: new, import, or add, into the selected folder
in the sidebar   ↑ ↓ → ← move and fold, ⏎ open, F2 rename, ⌘⌫ delete, a letter
                 jumps to a name (one no name starts with is the page's key); a
                 click that opens a row gives the keys back to the page
                 ⇧F10 (or the Menu key) opens the selected row's menu at the row;
                 the same on a tab rail's row and on a folder lens's row (Tab
                 reaches one, ⏎ opens it)
in a menu        ↓ ↑ move (round the ends), Home End, ⏎ or space runs the item,
                 → opens move to… and ← or esc leaves it (in its filter ← leaves
                 once nothing is typed), esc closes, a letter jumps to the next
                 item starting with it; while a menu is open every key is its
                 own, none the page's; closed, the focus is back where it was
⌘⇧N              new collection         ⌘O           import a folder as a collection
⌘⇧O              import…                ⌘⇧W          close collection
⌘V               an address, with nothing focused: clipped into the active tab's folder
⌘W               close tab              ⌘\           split pane
⌃Tab ⌃⇧Tab       the active pane's next tab and the one before (⌘⇧] ⌘⇧[ the same)
⌘⇧T              the tab closed last, open again (as long as the collection stays open)
⌃⌘S              sidebar                ⌘⇧I          inspector
⌘= ⌘−            text size, bigger and smaller (⌘0 back to 100%); kept with the settings
                 (over a book's pages they zoom the pages: see in a book)
⌘,               settings: theme, accent, density, text size, reopen the last vault on start,
                 download images when clipping, show remote images, queue
                 shows titles, focus stays within daily budget,
                 postpone backlog at start of day
                 (kept outside the vault); the day's budget,
                 where clippings go, and plugins (kept in the vault's .ilmi)

on the welcome   ⏎ open the selected collection, j k move, n new collection,
                 i import folder; a folder dropped on it is imported as a collection
                 F2 rename the selected collection (⏎ commits, Esc cancels), ⌘⌫ delete
                 it (asked on the row: Esc keeps); right-click, a long press, or the
                 row's ··· for the same two
⌘K               export collection · folder, export collection · zip: the collection
                 out as a folder of Markdown files, or as one zip

in a source      select, then e section (a clipping or a chapter: in its
                 note; an extract document in an older extract) or m make
                 a card; r start reading
in a note        ⌘B bold, ⌘I italic, ⌘E code, ⌘⇧X strike, ⌘K link (over a
                 selection; with none it is the palette), ⌘1–⌘6 heading, ⌘0
                 paragraph, ⌘⇧8 bullets, ⌘⇧7 numbers, ⌘⇧9 tasks, ⌘⇧. quote; in
                 a list Tab ⇧Tab ⌥⇧↑ ⌥⇧↓ move an item with its children, ⌘⏎
                 ticks a task; in code ⌘⏎ leaves the block, and so does ⏎
                 after its closing ``` typed on a line of its own; ⌘-click
                 opens a link or a bare address; with a selection ⌘⇧E
                 extract, ⌘⇧M make a card;
                 / at a block's start or after a space: insert (a heading,
                 a list, a quote, code, a formula, a rule, a table, an image,
                 a cloze, q&a, both ways), ↑↓ ⏎, esc keeps the /; ⌘⇧V paste
                 as plain text; an image pasted or dropped lands beside the
                 note as img-NN
in any page      ⌘F find (case, accents, and Arabic vowel marks folded), ⏎ ⇧⏎
                 or ⌘G ⌘⇧G the next and the one before; ⌥⌘F replace
in a book        a document with pages: its pages beside its notes (the
                 body, in the editor); drag on a page to box a passage
                 (again for another, any page; a text selection boxes its
                 lines), e makes it a section of the notes, m a section to
                 card in (or, where the page names no sections, a clip
                 under ## p. N), esc clears; ⏎ right after writes in it;
                 [ ] step the sections; ⌘\ hides the notes; / search
                 (⏎ the next hit, ⇧⏎ the one before, counted n / m;
                 n and N the same once the caret has left the field; the
                 hits stay lit until esc), g go to a page; ⏎ on a selected
                 reference such as (3.12) peeks at its target, and ⏎ again
                 or a press in the peek goes there; drag the margin to move
                 the read point;
                 a press on one of the PDF's own links follows it (a web
                 address opens in the browser); ⌥-press, or right-click
                 then peek, on a link or on a word that names something
                 ((3.12), Fig. 4, Theorem 2, Section 3.2, §3.2, [17]) peeks
                 at where it points without going;
                 f letters every link and reference in view: the letters
                 typed follow that one, with ⇧ on the last they peek, esc
                 or any other key lets them go;
                 t the PDF's contents in the palette (type to narrow, ⏎
                 goes);
                 ⌫ back to where the last jump was made from (a search's
                 first hit, g, the contents, a link, a reference, a section
                 more than a screen away), ⇧⌫ forward again;
                 right-click on a selection: section, card, search the web,
                 search scholar (in the browser);
                 ⌘= ⌘− zoom the pages in and out (fit, 125%, 150%, 200%,
                 250%, 300%), ⌘0 back to fit, a pinch on a trackpad or of
                 two fingers on the glass steps once; a book's zoom is kept
                 for the device; with the caret in the notes they are the
                 text size;
                 b a bookmark at the place in view: name it in the bar,
                 ⏎ keeps it, esc does not (a small mark in the left gutter;
                 right-click it to remove);
                 ⇧M then a letter marks the place in view, ' then the
                 letter goes to it; a lowercase letter is the book's, a
                 capital the collection's (it opens its book); any other
                 key lets the wait go;
                 h highlights the selection, or with none the words under
                 the boxes drawn (the box's bar has highlight too, and under
                 a finger look up: a held drag is a finger's box); h with
                 the selection, a box, or the last press inside a highlight
                 clears it; 1 2 3 4 right
                 after choose its ink (a band, a rule, a dashed rule, a
                 dotted rule); right-click on a highlight: section, card,
                 clear, the four inks;
                 ⇧P at a place, then ⇧P where it should show, ties the
                 first to the second (a portal: a small mark in the page's
                 right margin, a press on it peeks at where it shows, ⏎
                 goes; right-click it to remove); esc lets a half-made
                 one go;
                 v holds the line a third of the way down the view under a
                 rule (so does a press in a page's left margin, beside a
                 line); ↓ ↑ or j k move it a line, the page following;
                 r sets the read point to it; v or esc lets it go
in the palette   over a book: contents ·, bookmarks ·, highlights · (its
                 own), portals beside · (the book again in the pane
                 beside, following the portals of the page being read, into
                 another book when a portal leads there; run again, or
                 close that pane, to stop); anywhere:
                 bookmarks (every book's), marks (by letter)
in the intake    ⌘⏎ place & open, ⌘⇧⏎ place & leave it
in a deck        j k (or ↑ ↓) move, ⏎ or a double-click opens the card in the
                 card view, / search, m make a card; click selects, ⇧-click
                 a range, ⌘-click adds or drops one, ⌘A all shown, esc lets
                 the selection go; ⌫ deletes what is selected (with nothing
                 selected, the cursor's row), × on a row or right-click
                 deletes one, and a delete can be undone from its notice;
                 right-click moves a card to another deck
in a new card    ⌘1 q&a, ⌘2 bidir, ⌘3 cloze (the same in every card
                 editor), ⌘B a blank in a cloze; ⌘⏎ save, ⇧⌘⏎ save and
                 start another, esc cancel (in a deck, what was typed
                 comes back on the next m)
in the card view [ ] the card before and the next, o the card in its
                 document, s suspend the card or bring it back (undone from
                 its notice); in a face ⌘⏎ save, esc revert, ⌘1 ⌘2 ⌘3 the form
in a card's face Tab the other face (⇧Tab back); in a cell or a list it is the
                 editor's
in the numbers   on a day: ↑ ↓ a day, ← → a week, ⏎ or space its reviews (or
                 what comes due), esc closes them
in the queue     j k move (the row is brought into view), ⏎ enter (narrowed
                 to an exam: what is shown), o open one; f the focus, round
                 (all, recall, read, write; ⇧F back): the day narrowed to one
                 thing to do, ⏎ walking only that; a row names its file by its
                 path (`show titles in queue` in the settings brings the title back)
in a session     [ ] prev / next; space reveal, 1-4 grade (a card graded,
                 come back to: graded again in its grade's place); a card
                 graded into its steps (again; a new card good: due in
                 minutes) comes back before the session ends, after what
                 is left, as often as it takes, each time a review of its
                 own (an asking whose return was graded keeps its grade:
                 space is next); a card no
                 longer there: space or ] skip; s suspend the card (not
                 graded, on to the next, undone from its notice; come back
                 to, it says suspended, space is next and s brings it back);
                 o the card's source, in a tab of its own; ⏎ next (a book's: done for today; at its end:
                 dismiss), p later, d dismiss (both can be undone from the
                 notice; come back to, an element says what was done and ⏎
                 is on to the next); a key held down acts once
on a heading     right-click: come back (its section returns until you
                 dismiss it), dismiss, or bring back; the inspector and
                 the palette have the same
```
