Root families, one card per root: what the derived forms mean. Started in spring when Prof. Lindqvist made us do "root maps" for every new word. The vocab deck has the words; this has the system. Roots written in Arabic letters with hyphens on the front (that's how the handouts write them) and the forms on the back.

## patterns and weak roots

what does form VI typically carry? :: reciprocity (تَعَاوَنَ to cooperate) or pretending (تَجَاهَلَ to feign ignorance, تَمَارَضَ to feign illness) ^rt-form-vi
<!-- sr due=2026-11-06 st=review s=108.4 d=4 r=11 l=2 ivl=107 last=2026-07-22T07:01:40Z g=3 -->

what does form VII typically carry? :: passive / reflexive of I, never with a direct object: كَسَرَ → اِنْكَسَرَ to be broken; no VII for roots beginning ء و ي ل ن ر ^rt-form-vii
<!-- sr due=2026-12-17 st=review s=123.5 d=3.23 r=11 l=0 ivl=112 last=2026-08-27T08:10:13Z g=3 -->

root of اِتَّفَقَ? :: w-f-q, VIII — the w assimilates to the infixed t: *iwtafaqa → ittafaqa ^rt-ittafaqa
<!-- sr due=2027-01-10 st=review s=152.4 d=3.12 r=14 l=1 ivl=148 last=2026-08-15T19:23:28Z g=2 -->

root of اِدَّعَى? :: d-ʿ-w, VIII — the infixed t assimilates to the d: *idtaʿā → iddaʿā (to claim) ^rt-iddaa
<!-- sr due=2026-11-10 st=review s=13.2 d=4.43 r=5 l=0 ivl=12 last=2026-10-29T08:12:21Z g=3 -->

root of مِيزَانٌ? :: w-z-n — w becomes y after kasra: *miwzān → mīzān (the scale; also the grammarians' pattern-word) ^rt-mizan
<!-- sr due=2027-01-09 st=review s=107 d=4.79 r=10 l=0 ivl=107 last=2026-09-24T08:35:05Z g=2 -->

root of تُرَاثٌ? :: w-r-ṯ — initial w replaced by t (cf. وَرِثَ to inherit) ^rt-turath
<!-- sr due=2027-01-04 st=review s=85.4 d=4.45 r=9 l=0 ivl=89 last=2026-10-07T20:18:17Z g=2 -->

root of اِصْطَبَرَ? :: ṣ-b-r, VIII — t becomes ṭ after an emphatic: *iṣtabara → iṣṭabara ^rt-istabara
<!-- sr due=2026-11-05 st=review s=11.1 d=4.18 r=4 l=0 ivl=10 last=2026-10-26T20:49:37Z g=3 -->

root of مُخْتَار and why is it ambiguous? :: ḫ-y-r, VIII; the participle is the same for active (chooser) and passive (chosen) — *muḫtayir / muḫtayar both → muḫtār ^rt-mukhtar

root of قَاضٍ, and why the tanwīn under the ḍ? :: q-ḍ-y; defective active participle: *qāḍiyun → qāḍin (nom./gen.), acc. قَاضِيًا ^rt-qadin
