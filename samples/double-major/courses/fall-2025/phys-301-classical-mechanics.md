## things Lindgren kept repeating

Choose coordinates that make the constraints disappear. Then write $T$ and $V$ in those coordinates, and never touch a force again until someone asks you for one.

Every conserved quantity is a symmetry somebody hasn't told you about yet. (This is Noether, said loosely.)
