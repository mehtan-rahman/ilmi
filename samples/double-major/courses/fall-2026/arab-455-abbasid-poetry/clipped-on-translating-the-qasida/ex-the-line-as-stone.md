The classical critics praised a line that stood on its own and blamed one whose sense ran over into the next. The qaṣīda is built of complete lines the way a wall is built of stones, and the joints show. An English translation that runs the sense on from line to line, in the manner of blank verse, makes the poem read more smoothly and less like itself.
