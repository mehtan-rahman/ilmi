## proposal outline, as submitted 10-08

1. The qaṣīda and the court: tripartite form, the panegyric exchange (Ibn Qutayba; Stetkevych).
2. The muḥdaṯūn and the convention: Abū Nuwās's mock-nasīb, Abū Tammām's no-nasīb ode. The form as stage.
3. al-Mutanabbī's life in three courts: Aleppo, Fusṭāṭ, Shiraz.
4. Self-fashioning in the Sayfiyyāt: the al-Ḥadaṯ ode, the reproach mīmiyya.
5. Self-fashioning in the Kāfūriyyāt: the panegyrics, the double reading.
6. The satires and after.
7. Conclusion: the poet's voice as the qaṣīda's last subject.

Dropped 1–3 into the cut material (paper, bottom). Dropped Shiraz. Dropped "self-fashioning" from the title; keeping "voice" (Larkin's word, and hers). Answer to "whose?": the medieval commentators' first, then mine. That became §4 in v2.
