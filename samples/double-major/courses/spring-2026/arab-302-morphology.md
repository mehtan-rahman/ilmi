ARAB 302, Arabic morphology (الصَّرْف, al-ṣarf). Prof. Haddad, TTh 9:35–10:50, spring 2026. Final was Tue 5/5. Got an A-.

Unit notes are the children of this page (verb forms, weak verbs, maṣdar patterns and derived nouns). Problem sets were on paper and are not here. Everything I still review from this course is in the cards inside the unit notes.

Prof. Haddad's three rules, said every class:

Find the root first. Every word has one, and three-quarters of the errors are a wrong root.

The pattern is the meaning. فَاعِل is a doer, مَفْعُول a thing done to, مَفْعَل a place. Learn the pattern and you know words you have never seen.

Weak letters are not irregular, they are lazy. They drop or change by a dozen rules and every "exception" is one of them.
