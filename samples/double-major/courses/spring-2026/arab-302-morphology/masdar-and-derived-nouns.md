## the maṣdar of Form I: tendencies, not rules

The derived forms have one maṣdar pattern each (see verb forms). Form I has dozens, and the dictionary is the only authority. Prof. Haddad: "there are tendencies; the tendencies are what you guess with when the dictionary is not there". He gave about ten.

| pattern | tends to go with | examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| فَعْل | transitive faʿala | ضَرْب، فَهْم، قَتْل، فَتْح |
| فُعُول | intransitive faʿala | جُلُوس، خُرُوج، دُخُول، قُعُود |
| فَعَل | intransitive faʿila | فَرَح، تَعَب، عَطَش، غَضَب |
| فِعَالَة | crafts, professions, offices | زِرَاعَة، تِجَارَة، خِيَاطَة، سِفَارَة |
| فَعَلَان | movement, agitation | غَلَيَان، جَرَيَان، طَيَرَان، دَوَرَان |
| فُعَال | illness; sounds | سُعَال، صُدَاع، زُكَام، صُرَاخ |
| فَعِيل | sounds; departure | صَهِيل، هَدِير، رَحِيل |
| فِعَال | refusal, aversion | إِبَاء، نِفَار، شِرَاد |
| فُعْلَة | colors | حُمْرَة، زُرْقَة، صُفْرَة |
| فُعُولَة، فَعَالَة | qualities (faʿula) | سُهُولَة، صُعُوبَة، فَصَاحَة، بَلَاغَة |

Many verbs have more than one, with a meaning split: قَتْل "killing" vs قِتَال (Form III); ذَهَاب and ذُهُوب both from ذَهَبَ.

## other maṣdars

**maṣdar mīmī** (with a prefixed m-): same meaning as the regular maṣdar, a little more abstract or literary. Form I مَفْعَل: مَطْلَب "demand", مَذْهَب "way, school of thought", مَقْتَل "killing". For assimilated verbs مَفْعِل: مَوْعِد "appointment". For derived forms it looks like the passive participle: مُنْقَلَب "turning point; return", مُعْتَقَد "belief". [the participle, the place noun and the maṣdar mīmī of a derived form are all the same shape. Context only. This is the most confusing thing in the unit.]

**ism al-marra** (noun of one instance, nomen vicis): Form I فَعْلَة: جَلْسَة "a sitting, session", ضَرْبَة "a blow", نَظْرَة "a glance". Derived forms: the maṣdar + ة: اِنْطِلَاقَة "a start", اِبْتِسَامَة "a smile". If the maṣdar already ends in ة, add وَاحِدَة: إِقَامَة وَاحِدَة. These are what make the bayān al-ʿadad of 401's mafʿūl muṭlaq: قَفَزَ قَفْزَتَيْنِ.

**ism al-hayʾa** (noun of manner): Form I only, فِعْلَة: جِلْسَة "a way of sitting", مِشْيَة "gait". جَلَسَ جِلْسَةَ القُرْفُصَاءِ, "he sat squatting" — and that is a mafʿūl muṭlaq for bayān al-nawʿ.

**maṣdar ṣināʿī** (artificial): noun or adjective + -iyya: إِنْسَانِيَّة "humanity", حُرِّيَّة "freedom", اِشْتِرَاكِيَّة "socialism", وَطَنِيَّة "patriotism". Huge in MSA.

the Form I ism al-marra has the pattern {{c1::فَعْلَة}} (جَلْسَة), the ism al-hayʾa {{c2::فِعْلَة}} (جِلْسَة) ^sf-marra-haya

## ism al-makān and ism al-zamān

The noun of place and the noun of time share their patterns. Form I:

مَفْعَل when the imperfect is yafʿulu or yafʿalu, or the root is defective: مَكْتَب "office, desk" (yaktubu), مَلْعَب "playing field" (yalʿabu), مَطْعَم "restaurant" (yaṭʿamu), مَرْمًى "goal, target" (ramā, defective), مَلْهًى "place of amusement".

مَفْعِل when the imperfect is yafʿilu, or the root is assimilated: مَجْلِس "council, sitting place" (yajlisu), مَنْزِل "house, stopping place" (yanzilu), مَعْرِض "exhibition" (yaʿriḍu), مَوْعِد "appointed time/place", مَوْقِف "position, stop", مَوْلِد "birthplace, birthday" (time and place both).

The famous exceptions, mafʿil where mafʿal is expected: مَسْجِد (sajada yasjudu), مَشْرِق, مَغْرِب, مَطْلِع, مَسْكِن. [Haddad: "learn eleven exceptions and move on". I only have these five]

With ة: a place where something happens habitually or abounds: مَدْرَسَة, مَكْتَبَة "library, bookshop", مَطْبَعَة "printing press", مَقْبَرَة "cemetery", مَأْسَدَة "a place full of lions" [his favorite].

Derived forms: the passive participle shape: مُسْتَشْفًى "hospital" (X, š-f-y), مُلْتَقًى "meeting place" (VIII, l-q-y), مُجْتَمَع "society" (VIII, j-m-ʿ), مُنْتَزَه "park" (VIII, n-z-h).

the Form I noun of place is {{c1::مَفْعِل}} when the imperfect is yafʿilu or the root is assimilated (مَجْلِس، مَوْعِد) ^sf-mafil

## ism al-tafḍīl (the elative)

أَفْعَل for both comparative and superlative: أَكْبَر "bigger, biggest", أَحْسَن "better, best". Diptote (mamnūʿ min al-ṣarf): أَكْبَرُ, no tanwīn.

Three constructions:

With min, indefinite, invariable in gender and number: هِيَ أَكْبَرُ مِنْ أُخْتِهَا, "she is older than her sister" (not *kubrā min).

With al-, definite, agrees: الأَكْبَرُ, الكُبْرَى (fem. فُعْلَى), الأَكَابِر / الكُبَر (pl.). المَسْجِدُ الأَكْبَرُ, المَدِينَةُ الكُبْرَى.

In iḍāfa: with a definite noun, superlative, may agree or not: أَكْبَرُ الطُّلَّابِ, "the oldest of the students"; with an indefinite singular, "the most… of any": أَكْبَرُ طَالِبٍ.

Weak and doubled roots: أَعْلَى (ʿ-l-w, final weak → ى), أَدْنَى, أَقْوَى; doubled أَشَدّ (not *ašdad), أَقَلّ, أَحَبّ. خَيْر and شَرّ drop their hamza: هُوَ خَيْرٌ مِنْكَ, not *aḵyar (aḵyar exists but is rare).

Can't be formed directly from: colors and defects (they are already afʿal), derived-form verbs, passive meanings, verbs that don't admit degree. Then: أَشَدّ / أَكْثَر / أَقَلّ + the maṣdar as tamyīz: أَشَدُّ حُمْرَةً "redder", أَكْثَرُ اسْتِخْدَامًا "more used". [ARAB 401: this is tamyīz al-nisba; see ḥāl and tamyīz note]

The feminine of أَكْبَر is {{c1::كُبْرَى}}, pattern {{c2::فُعْلَى}} ^sf-kubra

## ṣifa mušabbaha and intensive forms

**al-ṣifa al-mušabbaha** ("the adjective resembling the participle"): a permanent or inherent quality, as opposed to the active participle's event. Mostly from faʿula and faʿila verbs. Patterns:

| pattern | examples |
| --- | --- |
| فَعِيل | كَبِير، صَغِير، جَمِيل، كَرِيم |
| فَعِل | فَرِح، تَعِب، قَلِق |
| فَعْل | صَعْب، سَهْل، ضَخْم |
| أَفْعَل / فَعْلَاء / فُعْل | أَحْمَر حَمْرَاء حُمْر؛ أَعْمَى عَمْيَاء عُمْي (colors and defects) |
| فَعْلَان / فَعْلَى | عَطْشَان عَطْشَى؛ غَضْبَان غَضْبَى؛ جَوْعَان (hunger, thirst, emotions) |

Contrast: فَارِح is not a word in use; فَرِح is. حَاسِن doesn't exist next to حَسَن. The ism al-fāʿil of a faʿula verb is unused because the quality has no "event".

**ṣiyaġ al-mubālaġa** (intensive forms), only from transitive Form I verbs, "doing X a lot, being very X":

| pattern | examples |
| --- | --- |
| فَعَّال | كَذَّاب "great liar", أَكَّال "glutton", فَتَّاح |
| فَعُول | صَبُور "very patient", غَفُور "much-forgiving", شَكُور |
| مِفْعَال | مِقْدَام "very bold", مِعْطَاء "generous" |
| فَعِيل | رَحِيم، سَمِيع، عَلِيم |
| فَعِل | حَذِر "wary" |

Overlap: فَعِيل is both ṣifa mušabbaha (كَبِير) and intensive (رَحِيم); context and verb type decide. [exam: "classify رَحِيم" — answer was mubālaġa, because raḥima is transitive and the meaning is "much mercy", not a quality like size]

the adjectives of colors and defects have the pattern {{c1::أَفْعَل}}, feminine {{c2::فَعْلَاء}}, plural فُعْل ^sf-afal-fala
