Dyson was careful to call this a physical argument, not a proof, and the details have been debated since. But the conclusion has held up, and the structure of the argument has turned out to be general: whenever flipping the sign of a coupling makes a system unstable, the perturbation series in that coupling diverges.

[mine] The chain, so I can say it in OH: convergent series in $e^2$ ⇒ analytic in a disk ⇒ defined for small $e^2 < 0$ ⇒ like charges attract ⇒ $N$ pairs in two clusters have energy $\sim 2Nmc^2 - CN^2$, unbounded below ⇒ no vacuum ⇒ not analytic at $0$ ⇒ radius zero. The toy integral is the same shape: at $g < 0$ the $e^{-gx^4}$ blows up and $Z$ doesn't exist.
