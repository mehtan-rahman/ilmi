اِخْتَصَمَ رَجُلَانِ إِلَى قَاضٍ مِنْ قُضَاةِ البَصْرَةِ فِي كِيسٍ فِيهِ أَلْفُ دِرْهَمٍ. فَقَالَ أَحَدُهُمَا: «أَيُّهَا القَاضِي، أَوْدَعْتُ هٰذَا الرَّجُلَ أَلْفَ دِرْهَمٍ قَبْلَ سَفَرِي، فَلَمَّا رَجَعْتُ جَحَدَنِي.» وَقَالَ الآخَرُ: «مَا أَوْدَعَنِي شَيْئًا، وَلَا رَأَيْتُ لَهُ دِرْهَمًا قَطُّ.»

## translation (prepared for class, 10 Sept)

Two men brought a dispute before one of the judges of Basra over a purse containing a thousand dirhams. One of them said, "Judge, I deposited a thousand dirhams with this man before I travelled, and when I came back he denied it." The other said, "He deposited nothing with me, and I have never set eyes on a single dirham of his."

Dr. Saeed on "denied it": جَحَدَنِي is literally "he denied me," i.e. denied my claim / my right. English needs an "it." Keep "denied" rather than "refused" — جَحَدَ is denying something you know is true, which is exactly what the story is about. "Set eyes on" is mine, she said fine but don't over-colour sight passages.

## parsing

- اِخْتَصَمَ — VIII perfect 3ms, singular before the dual subject رَجُلَانِ (nom. dual)
- إِلَى قَاضٍ — gen. of the defective participle: قَاضٍ for *qāḍiyin*; with the article القَاضِي
- مِنْ قُضَاةِ البَصْرَةِ — partitive مِنْ; قُضَاة broken pl. pattern fuʿala (cf. رُمَاة, دُعَاة)
- فِي كِيسٍ فِيهِ أَلْفُ دِرْهَمٍ — فِي "concerning"; فِيهِ أَلْفُ دِرْهَمٍ is a jumla ṣifa on indefinite كِيس (khabar muqaddam فِيهِ + mubtadaʾ أَلْفُ)
- أَيُّهَا القَاضِي — vocative via ayyuhā, nom.
- أَوْدَعْتُ هٰذَا الرَّجُلَ أَلْفَ دِرْهَمٍ — two objects: person then thing
- قَبْلَ سَفَرِي — ẓarf + iḍāfa; maṣdar سَفَر + my
- فَلَمَّا رَجَعْتُ جَحَدَنِي — lammā + perf, main clause perf
- مَا أَوْدَعَنِي شَيْئًا — mā + perf = past negative; ـنِي with nūn al-wiqāya
- وَلَا رَأَيْتُ … قَطُّ — "nor"; qaṭṭu only after negated perfect
