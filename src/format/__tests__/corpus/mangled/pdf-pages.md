A chapter of a book whose pages are another document's file: `pdf` names the book's PDF by a path, and `pages` says which of its pages this document is, numbered as the book numbers them. The first `pages` is the field, written back as `pages: 2`; `0-1`, `9-3`, the empty one and `two` are raw lines, each with a diagnostic, kept as written.

pages: 3-4 in the body is prose.
