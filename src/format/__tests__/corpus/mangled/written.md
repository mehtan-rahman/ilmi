Written notes (§3): `written` is the notebook's id, `paper` what its sheets are ruled with, `sheets` its pages in order. The first of each is the field, written back canonically (`sheets: 1 2 5`, the comment dropped); an id in capitals or too short, a paper that is not one of the four, the empty one, a sheet twice, a sheet 0, commas and a word are raw lines, each with a diagnostic, kept as written. The body of a notebook is kept and not shown.
