## ch.4 — radical empiricism

The chapter’s wager is that relations between things are themselves part of experience — felt directly, not inferred and pasted on afterward by the mind. If that is true, the join between two ideas is as real as the ideas.

radical empiricism holds that the {{c1::relations}} between things are themselves {{c1::directly experienced}}, not added by the mind ^cprag-rel
