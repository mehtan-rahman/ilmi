## §1 — on slow reading

The margin is the only honest measure of reading. A page you passed your eyes over leaves nothing there; a page you argued with is fenced in ink. The notebook treats an empty margin as an unread page, regardless of how long you spent on it.

## §2 — the margin as workshop

The margin is where understanding crystallizes — not because the space is special but because it forces reduction. You cannot fit the paragraph back into the margin; you can only fit what you took from it.

transcription is not understanding; the margin forces a {{reduction}} into your own words ^cmar-margin
