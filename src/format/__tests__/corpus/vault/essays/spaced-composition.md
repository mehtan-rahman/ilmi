## working title

A note arguing with itself about whether writing can be scheduled the way reading and review already are. Keep it short until it earns length.

## §1 — the thesis

Reading has a loop and review has a loop; writing has deadlines and guilt. The wager here is that a draft is just another element that should come back on a schedule — not to be graded, only to be touched again — until the writer decides it is finished.

The danger is over-design. The loop is not a workflow; it is a door that opens, lets you do anything, and closes. Resist adding steps to it.

Every constraint added to the writing loop is one more reason not to enter it. The empty afternoon is the feature, not the bug. [tighten this — it is the crux of the piece]
