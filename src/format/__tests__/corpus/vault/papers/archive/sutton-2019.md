## opening — where humans got it wrong

The bitter lesson, restated for a notebook: methods that scale with computation eventually overtake methods that encode what a clever human believes about the problem. The believing feels like progress and is mostly a tax.

Search and learning are the two things that scale. Everything else is scaffolding we are reluctant to throw away because we built it.
