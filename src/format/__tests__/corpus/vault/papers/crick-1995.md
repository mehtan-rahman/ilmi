## intro — what consciousness is not

Before we ask what awareness is, the notebook insists, we have to be ruthless about what it is not — most of the early confusion comes from smuggling the answer into the question.

Consciousness is not a single thing, and it is not the whole of mental life. Most of what the brain does, it does in the dark. The interesting question is the narrow one: why is some small fraction of that processing accompanied by experience at all?

## three eliminations

The first elimination is the homunculus. There is no inner viewer watching a screen; if there were, we would only have moved the problem inward. The regress is the tell.

The second elimination is language. A creature need not be able to report an experience in order to have one. Tying awareness to report quietly excludes infants, animals, and most of one’s own waking life.

What remains is a tractable target: the smallest set of neural events sufficient for one specific conscious percept. Name it and you can study it.
