# Maxwell's equations

Four equations, in differential form and SI units, say everything classical about the electric and magnetic fields. Two of them are tested below, one as a right-hand side and one as a whole line.

$$\begin{align}
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} &= {{c1::\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}}} \\
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} &= 0 \\
{{c2::\nabla \times \mathbf{E} &= -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}}} \\
\nabla \times \mathbf{B} &= \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}
\end{align}$$ ^cmx-field
