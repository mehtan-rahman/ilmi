Templates and scopes

A template language writes its slots as {{name}} and {{ count }}, and a scope resolution in C++ is written std::vector or, with spaces for the eye, std :: vector. A note on ratios: the odds were 3 :: 1 in the end, and a triple ::: marks a fence in some dialects. The paragraph has enough words around these marks to read as the article's prose rather than as a stray line of chrome.

A question mark can stand alone on a line of a poem:
?
and then the poem goes on, which is a thing poems do when a page is clipped.

A caret at the end of a line names a footnote in some wikis ^note and a comment opens with <!-- in HTML, which a page about HTML will say out loud more than once.

The prose otherwise goes on long enough for the page to be read as an article, with sentences, commas, and the ordinary rhythm of a post that explains a thing to a reader who came for it.
